# A scheme or return claim that fails because the batch was never written down

Original fictional example, not a real brand claim. A fictional retailer returns 30 cartons of short-dated stock worth Rs 640 each: 30 x 640 = Rs 19,200. Six weeks later the distributor raises the claim with the brand from memory, with no batch number and no photo. The brand closes it as late and untraceable, and the Rs 19,200 stays stuck while the distributor pays interest on working-capital borrowing.

Claim windows and documents differ by brand. Check your own agreement; do not assume any rule here applies to you.

What we do: open a claim line the day the return is recorded, with invoice number, batch, expiry date and a dated photo together. Review open claims every Friday, oldest first, and mark each one as raised, accepted or rejected.

Do not claim Bolcash supports goods returns or brand claims; it is not an approved feature. These are manual practices, not guaranteed recoveries.
