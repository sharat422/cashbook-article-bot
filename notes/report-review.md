# Check a period report against entries

Original fictional reporting example, not financial advice, an audit or a tax-return method.

A fictional October expense list contains rent of Rs 4,500, delivery costs of Rs 800 and packing supplies of Rs 700. The illustrative recorded total is 4,500 + 800 + 700 = 6,000. This is an expense total for the specified records, not profit, available cash or a tax deduction.

Suggested review: identify the report period, inspect which entries are included, check transaction dates and amounts, compare the sum with the underlying records and investigate a missing or duplicated item. If a November expense was included by mistake, correct the period interpretation before comparing totals.

If a record is pending confirmation or disputed, understand how it is represented before treating the report as a settled position. These notes do not establish Bolcash's treatment of pending records or the exact filters available. Describe these checks as questions to ask while inspecting the report, not a tested app screen sequence.

Bolcash has owner-approved reports based on recorded business data. Do not invent PDF or spreadsheet export, automated tax filing, live bank balances or a specific financial statement. A guide can encourage checking the inputs and period while explaining that a report cannot establish the truth of an incorrectly entered transaction. No forecast, savings percentage or guarantee of error-free reporting is supported.
