# Check the credit limit at billing, not at month-end

Original fictional example, not a real distributor. A fictional retailer has a credit limit of Rs 50,000 and Rs 46,000 already outstanding. A salesman, who is measured on sales, wants to bill another Rs 12,000. New outstanding would be 46,000 + 12,000 = Rs 58,000, which is Rs 8,000 over the limit.

Receivables grow faster than sales when a slow payer keeps being billed.

What we do: check the limit when the bill is raised. If the limit would be crossed, collect first, then bill. In this example Rs 8,000 must be collected: 46,000 - 8,000 = 38,000, and 38,000 + 12,000 = 50,000. A salesman cannot override the limit alone; the owner or accounts lead approves exceptions, and the exception is written down.

Do not claim Bolcash enforces credit limits; that is not an approved feature. Limits themselves are a business decision. This is a practice to consider, not a guaranteed way to reduce bad debt.
