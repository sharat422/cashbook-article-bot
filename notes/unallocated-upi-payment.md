# A UPI payment that does not say which bill it is for

Original fictional example, not a real customer story. A fictional retailer has three open bills: A for Rs 6,200 (oldest), B for Rs 8,800 and C for Rs 7,800. Total outstanding: 6,200 + 8,800 + 7,800 = 22,800. The retailer sends Rs 15,000 by UPI with no note.

If staff apply it to the newest bills first, C (7,800) is cleared and the other 7,200 goes to B, leaving B at 1,600. Bill A, the oldest at Rs 6,200, still shows open and overdue, and a reminder goes out for a bill the retailer believes was paid. The retailer actually meant A and B: 6,200 + 8,800 = 15,000. Open balance is the same either way, 22,800 - 15,000 = 7,800, but the wrong bills look overdue.

What we do: call the retailer the same day, ask which bills the payment is for, and write the bill numbers next to the UPI reference. Until confirmed, keep the payment as "received, not allocated" rather than guessing. Do not claim Bolcash matches UPI payments to bills automatically or confirms UPI receipts. This is a manual practice, not a guaranteed result.
