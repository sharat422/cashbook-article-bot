# Review the answer to a spoken business query

Original fictional example, not a claim about a particular model response or live account data.

A fictional set of three recorded expenses for one day is Rs 200, Rs 300 and Rs 450. The sum is 200 + 300 + 450 = 950. Before using an answer to a spoken query about expenses, check which day or period the question concerned and compare the answer with the relevant entries. An answer about the wrong date can sound fluent while addressing a different question.

Suggested checks: what was asked, whose records were intended, date range, whether an amount means income or expenses, and whether an entry is duplicated or missing. If the words captured differ from the intended question, restate the query or check the records directly. Do not treat conversational confidence as proof of accuracy.

The owner approved getting business details by speaking in Bolcash. These notes do not establish every supported query, every language, real-time bank access, predictive insights or the ability to change records by speaking. The example only supports a narrow discussion of reviewing a query and its answer.

Use fictional amounts and anonymised labels when illustrating queries in a public guide. Do not expose a real customer's balances or identifying details. This is a proposed review habit, not a promise of perfect answers, automatic bookkeeping or a measurable time saving.
