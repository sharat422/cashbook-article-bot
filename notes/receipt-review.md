# Review a scanned receipt before using its details

Original fictional receipt exercise. It does not establish OCR accuracy rates, supported languages or automatic saving behaviour.

A fictional packing-supply receipt lists Rs 450 for boxes, Rs 320 for tape and Rs 230 for wrapping material. The illustrative total is 450 + 320 + 230 = 1,000. No GST or other tax calculation is implied. If the scanned output reads Rs 1,800, compare it with the actual receipt rather than accepting the OCR result because it looks plausible.

Suggested review fields: merchant text, receipt date, item descriptions, amounts, total and any payment-status wording. Check a decimal point and the difference between a date and an invoice number. Do not invent missing merchant names or item amounts. Mark an unreadable field for manual checking.

A receipt with printed payment information is not by itself proof that money settled in a bank account. Confirm actual payment separately where needed. Retain only receipt information the business needs; do not publish a customer's identifying details in an article example.
