# Never overwrite a figure in the ledger

Original fictional example, not a real dispute. In a fictional retailer's bahi the opening balance is Rs 30,000 and a payment of Rs 15,800 was received, so the balance is 30,000 - 15,800 = Rs 14,200. The payment was first written as Rs 12,400, then scribbled over and rewritten. Later the retailer points at the overwritten figure and says the balance is wrong. The distributor cannot show what the original entry was, and the argument damages the relationship more than the amount.

What we do: never overwrite. Draw one line through the wrong figure so it stays readable, write the correct entry below it, and add the date and initials. Ask the retailer to sign a monthly statement of the balance.

The same rule applies in a spreadsheet: add a correcting line instead of editing history. Do not claim Bolcash keeps a tamper-proof history; that is not an approved feature. These are practices to reduce disputes, not guaranteed outcomes.
