# Keep payments and accepted returns separate

Original fictional teaching material, not a customer case, legal advice or evidence of product capabilities.

A fictional customer owes Rs 12,000. The distributor confirms Rs 4,200 received and separately accepts Rs 800 of returned goods. The recorded balance is 12,000 - 4,200 - 800 = 7,000. The payment is money received. The accepted return is an adjustment, not another payment.

Suggested paper-ledger layout: date, customer code, entry type, amount, reference, status and remaining balance. Show the payment and return on separate lines. The running balance after the payment is Rs 7,800, then Rs 7,000 after acceptance of the return. Keep the reason and acceptance note with the return line.

In an alternative fictional case the Rs 800 return is disputed. Note the dispute without silently deducting it. The ledger remains Rs 7,800 pending acceptance; this does not mean both parties agree with the total. A reminder should describe the unresolved issue accurately.

Before entering a return adjustment, check which customer and original transaction it relates to, the amount being accepted and whether acceptance is actually recorded. Do not describe this as a tax-credit-note procedure. No legal or tax requirements are asserted. These notes do not establish goods-return support in Bolcash. Product claims must come only from app_facts.json. Do not guarantee fewer disputes or faster collections.
