# A payment message is a prompt to check

Original fictional example, not banking, legal or fraud-detection advice.

A fictional retailer owes Rs 6,500 and says Rs 3,500 has been transferred. Until the business confirms that payment in its actual payment records, the message alone does not justify marking the money received. If Rs 3,500 is then confirmed for the correct customer, the recorded balance becomes 6,500 - 3,500 = 3,000.

Suggested notes while checking: date of the claim, amount, customer reference and whether confirmation is pending or complete. A reference number is a clue for matching records, not by itself a guarantee that a transaction settled. A screenshot may omit details and should not replace checking the actual payment record.

Separate cash, bank and UPI descriptions where those differences are relevant to the business's records, without presenting any particular app setting as tested. If information is missing or disputed, preserve the question and seek clarification rather than inventing a receipt.

The owner has not approved automatic UPI confirmation or automatic bank reconciliation as marketing claims. These notes must not imply Bolcash independently verifies a bank transfer. They also do not establish a payment-reference input field, attachment feature or bank integration. This guide should teach the difference between a payment claim and a confirmed record, not promise that a tool removes the need for review.
